---
type: improvement
title: Compare chart depths in a responsive workspace
---
The chart editor sits beside a 3D lake bed and a flat depth map in compact, full-height panes with thin dividers and dense toolbars. On smaller screens the panels stack in reading order. Tracing no longer scrolls the page, and automatic vertical exaggeration makes wide, shallow basins easier to inspect. A surface-style toggle switches between an extruded 3D stack and the smooth DEM. The stack uses the main 3D renderer with wood grain, lighting, shadows and an exploded view of twelve representative sheets on a rectangular backing, independently of project settings. Custom Data no longer shows the terrain generation dock.

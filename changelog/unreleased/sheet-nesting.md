---
type: feature
title: Nest your pieces onto stock sheets
---
In **Export**, choose **Nested sheets**, set your sheet size and click **Nest parts**. TopoStack packs every piece onto as few sheets as it can, moving and turning them to fit, and exports one file per sheet with each piece's layer id engraved where it stays hidden. Nesting runs in your browser with the open-source sparrow nesting engine, and the layout is still there after a reload.

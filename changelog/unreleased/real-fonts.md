type: feature
title: Real typefaces for labels and titles
---
Choose from eight new engraving fonts: four single-line fonts (Hershey Sans, Serif and Script, Relief SingleLine) that trace each letter once, and four filled typefaces (Jost, Oswald, Lora, Roboto Slab). They support lowercase and most accented letters. The title can now have its own font. See [map details](/guides/map-details).

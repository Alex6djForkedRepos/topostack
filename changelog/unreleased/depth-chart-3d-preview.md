type: feature
title: Verify traced depth charts in 3D
---
Depth charts now show an interactive 3D lake bed after tracing. Rotate, zoom, reset the view and adjust vertical exaggeration to compare the generated basin with your chart before keeping it. Switch to the north-up 2D depth map for another view; it also remains available when 3D cannot render. Changed inputs mark the preview as out of date.

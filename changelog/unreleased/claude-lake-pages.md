title: A page for every larger surveyed lake
---
More than 2,700 lakes now have a page of their own, such as [Crater Lake](/lake/crater-lake-oregon). Each one lists the survey's facts and source, the map scale at 12, 16 and 24 inches, and nearby lakes with depth data, and links straight into the studio. Find them from the [lake depth maps](/lakes) by region.

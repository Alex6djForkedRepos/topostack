type: feature
title: Place and size the north arrow, title and scale bar anywhere
---
The north arrow, title and scale bar are no longer limited to fixed spots. Choose **Move on preview**: the preview looks straight down on your finished piece, and you drag them anywhere, even across layers, or nudge them with the arrow keys. Drag the corner handle, or press + and −, to resize the north arrow or the title in place. Choose **Done** to engrave them where you left them, or **Cancel** to put them back. See [map details](/guides/map-details).

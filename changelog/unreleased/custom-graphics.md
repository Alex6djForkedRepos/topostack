---
type: feature
title: Place your own graphics on the piece
---
Upload an SVG logo, badge or decoration under **Graphics** in the Custom data view, then place it on the piece from a top-down view of the 3D stack: drag to move it, resize it from the corner and turn it from the grip above it. Each placed graphic can be engraved as a filled shape, scored as an outline, or cut out of the sheet it sits on so the sheet below shows through. See [custom graphics](/guides/custom-graphics).

title: Guides for custom data and depth charts
---
Two new guides cover the **Custom data** view. [Bring your own data](/guides/custom-data) explains what each section adds, how markers, paths, graphics and charts are saved and shared, and the limits at a glance. [Carve a lake from a depth chart](/guides/trace-a-depth-chart) walks through tracing a scanned, photographed or PDF chart, from choosing the lake to regenerating terrain with it.

The studio tour, settings reference, lake and troubleshooting guides now point to both.
